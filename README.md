Salmon Vision is on a mission to democratize advanced monitoring technology for salmon conservation across the Pacific Northwest.

Counting fish by hand means people watching hours of underwater video and sonar footage. Salmon Vision automates that work: cameras and sonar units at remote river sites capture footage, computer vision models detect, track, and identify fish, and field teams review the results and follow population counts, species composition, and migration timing on live dashboards.

## Partners

Salmon Vision is built with and for the people who monitor salmon rivers: First Nations fisheries programs and stewardship authorities, government agencies, and research and conservation organizations across British Columbia and Alaska. Core partners include the [Pacific Salmon Foundation](https://psf.ca), [Wild Salmon Center](https://wildsalmoncenter.org), and Lumax Ecological Analytics.

See the full list of partners at [salmonvision.org/partners](https://salmonvision.org/partners/).
